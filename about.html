About Us

Making Room for Inspiration — One Line Upon Line at a Time

Line Upon Line Co began in 2018 as a small creative venture between Emily and her friend Alexis. What started as a simple idea has grown into a family‑run business dedicated to creating faith‑inspired products that help individuals deepen their relationship with Jesus Christ.

In 2026, Line Upon Line became a fully family‑owned company, lovingly operated by Emily and her husband, Robert.

Our Family Story

Emily and Robert are high‑school sweethearts who were sealed in the Salt Lake Temple in 2009 — a place they had both dreamed of being married. Today, they share four wonderful children who bring joy, purpose, and a whole lot of laughter into their home.

They love a good hamburger, sci‑fi shows, and talking so much that they’re often late to things. Their life is full, warm, and centered on faith — and Line Upon Line is an extension of that.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to help you make room for inspiration. We believe that creating intentional spaces — in your home, in your study, and in your heart — invites the Spirit and strengthens your relationship with Jesus Christ.

The Lord has impressed upon our minds the importance of consecrating this work to Him. Everything we create is designed to share light, truth, and beauty.

What We Value

  • Faith at the center of every product
  • Intentional design that elevates worship and study
  • Quality craftsmanship you can feel
  • Small‑batch production with care and purpose
  • A desire to uplift every customer who visits our shop

Why We Create

We believe that the tools you use in your spiritual life should feel meaningful, beautiful, and inspired. Whether it’s a journal, a scripture accessory, or a bag designed for worship, our hope is that each item helps you draw closer to Christ.

Join Us on the Journey

We’re grateful you’re here. Explore our collections, find tools that speak to your heart, and make room for inspiration in your everyday life.
